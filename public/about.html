About InnoState

The People Behind the Properties

Twelve years, one city, and a reputation built one handshake at a time.

Built Different, On Purpose

InnoState started in 2014 as a two-person office above a coffee shop. We never wanted to be the biggest agency in town - we wanted to be the one people recommend to their parents. Today our team handles sales, rentals, and management across the city, but every client still gets a named agent who answers their own phone.

We work a deliberately small portfolio so nothing gets listed that we wouldn't view ourselves. If a property has a problem, you'll hear it from us first.
